# Project status

## Project type

A local-first toolkit for turning research material into an evidence-led decision packet.

## Current public state

Maintained. Release notes, version tags, and the validation workflow are the source of truth for published versions.

## Intended use

Use it to structure evidence, compare options, record assumptions, and prepare a reviewable decision packet.

## Not a replacement for

It does not establish facts, replace domain review, approve a decision, or transfer accountability from the decision-maker.

## Feedback and maintenance

Report reproducible defects or documentation gaps through GitHub Issue. Do not include personal data, credentials, or confidential material.

## Public boundary

Examples are fictional or de-identified. They show a reusable decision structure, not a client engagement or published policy conclusion.
